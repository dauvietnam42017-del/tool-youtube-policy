tool youtube

A private desktop tool for managing the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the channel owner has produced and reviewed to the owner's own YouTube channels (YouTube Data API v3).
  • Sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists for those videos.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time, click-through rate, audience retention, estimated revenue) through the YouTube Analytics API, to show them on a local dashboard.

Who can use it

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public, and only the Google accounts that own the managed channels sign in to it.
